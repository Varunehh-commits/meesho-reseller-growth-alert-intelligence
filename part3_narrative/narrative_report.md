## 3.3 Chart-choice Justification

### 1. Which month had the highest total revenue?

**Recommended chart: Bar chart**

This is a **univariate comparison across months** of total revenue. A bar chart makes the comparison clear within 10 seconds and should use a y-axis starting at zero. No 3D effect is needed, and no legend is necessary because there is only one series. The verified values are April = INR 419417.43, May = INR 444594.25, and June = INR 398055.24, so May had the highest total revenue.

### 2. What percentage share does Ethnic Wear represent of April's total revenue?

**Recommended chart: Donut chart**

This is a **part-to-whole relationship**: Ethnic Wear revenue of INR 104520.77 compared with April's total revenue of INR 419417.43, representing **24.92%**. A simple donut chart can communicate the share quickly within 10 seconds. It should remain simple and avoid unnecessary 3D effects.

### 3. How do the four regions compare on total revenue?

**Recommended chart: Bar chart**

This is a **univariate comparison across four regions** using total revenue. A bar chart makes the relative differences easy to compare within 10 seconds and should use a y-axis starting at zero. Because there is only one revenue series, a legend is not required. The chart should avoid 3D effects.
